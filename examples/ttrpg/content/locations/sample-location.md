# Village of Barovia

The first settlement encountered upon entering the mist-shrouded valley.

## Notable Locations

- Blood of the Vine Tavern
- Burgomaster's Manor
- Church of St. Andral

## Atmosphere

A grim, gray village where the sun never truly shines through the perpetual fog.
