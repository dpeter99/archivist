# Ireena Kolyana

Ireena is the adopted daughter of the village burgomaster, Kolyan Indirovich.

## Description

A striking young woman with auburn hair and piercing blue eyes. She bears a haunted look from recent events.

## Relationships

- Father: Kolyan Indirovich (deceased)
- Brother: Ismark the Lesser

## Notes

The party first encountered Ireena in the village of Barovia.
